I hate changes, I hate when things that I used to love, not feels
colorless. My life feels like a canvas ready to be paint, but without
nothing important to draw.

I write things that no one cares, and no body will care. Things that are
in my head and needs to go out. But just empty words that cover a negligible space
in the universe.

I heard once that we are one of the most exquisite worm food. Creatures born only
to suffer from existence. Searching a purpose that feels very far away. A goal that
never will reach.

I wonder what I should do. When death is not a possibility. How to suffer on the most
painless way possible. What I should do?

Now breath, feeling that things don't matter, just to wake up knowing that my existence
can only be invisible in the pass of time.

When words are not enough how we can express what we feel. How to express the pain that
remains invisible, the pain that we can deal, but never stop.

At least live has many ways to stop those feelings. Ways to silence the voice that
remind us how inferior we are. But even that relieve fade away. Nothing feels enough,
nothing feels important.

I hope that my suffering being something that will create something more beautiful, even
if is just a false dream, a lie that I told me just to have something to grab.

Just forgive me. I just want to cry.
